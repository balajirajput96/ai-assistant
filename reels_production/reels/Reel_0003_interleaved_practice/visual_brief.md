# Reel 0003 Visual Brief — Interleaved Practice

## Production choice

The current daily AI-image quota is unavailable. Rather than represent missing AI images as completed, Reel 0003 will use an original, topic-specific **motion-graphics visual set** generated locally from source-controlled geometry. It is designed for an educational 9:16 reel and contains no text; Hindi captions are burned during final assembly.

| Scene | Visual job | Conceptual treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contrast blocked practice with recognition | A single colour of repeated cards moves through one easy linear path. |
| 2 | Show interleaving | Blue, amber and coral cards alternate across multiple paths, visually signalling A–B–C switching. |
| 3 | Show method selection | Several related paths converge into a highlighted decision point, representing identifying the right method. |
| 4 | Show a bounded mini-set plan | A balanced six-card sequence repeats in an orderly practice grid, rather than random mixing. |

## Style

The art direction uses deep navy, teal, warm amber, coral and mint on a dark cinematic gradient. Soft glowing elements, rounded cards, and light motion during encoding create a polished science-and-learning visual language. Lower-centre space stays clear for captions.
